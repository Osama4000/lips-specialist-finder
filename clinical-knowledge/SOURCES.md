# Clinical knowledge sources

The routing ontology is deliberately hybrid:

1. **Live LIPS directory data** supplies current specialties, sub-specialties, conditions, expertise, clinician biography evidence and locations.
2. **Curated symptom concepts and common-language synonyms** broaden what call-centre staff can type when the exact term is not present in the LIPS taxonomy.
3. **NHS and NICE public guidance** informs selected synonym sets and safety/red-flag routing. It is not copied as diagnostic logic and does not replace LIPS clinical governance.

Key public references used when curating v6 include:

- LIPS specialist directory: https://lips.org.uk/our-specialists
- LIPS conditions & procedures: https://lips.org.uk/conditions-and-procedures/
- LIPS Spine & Neurosurgery: https://lips.org.uk/specialities/spine-and-neurosurgery
- NICE NG59, low back pain and sciatica: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng59
- NICE NG127, suspected neurological conditions: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng127
- NICE CG150, headaches: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg150
- NHS stroke symptoms: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/
- NHS heart palpitations: https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/heart-palpitations/
- NHS heart attack: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heart-attack/
- NHS anaphylaxis: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/anaphylaxis/
- NHS epilepsy: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/epilepsy/
- NHS dizziness: https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/dizziness/

The red-flag set must be reviewed and signed off against the organisation's own contact-centre escalation policy before operational clinical use.
